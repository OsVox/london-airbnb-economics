# Sources and provenance

- [Inside Airbnb downloads](https://insideairbnb.com/get-the-data/): current public London release labelled September 17, 2026. [Exact detailed file](https://data.insideairbnb.com/united-kingdom/england/london/2026-09-17/data/listings.csv.gz). Anonymous download verified, 48,282,891 compressed bytes; SHA-256 in logs/download.json. The actual last_scraped fields span September 18–23, 2026, so the filename date is a release label rather than every row's observation date.
- [Licence](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/): Inside Airbnb distributes these data under CC BY 4.0. Attribution: Inside Airbnb, London release September 2026, accessed October 16, 2026. Underlying observations are public Airbnb advertisements compiled by an independent third party. It is not an Airbnb-endorsed dataset.
- [Assumptions](https://insideairbnb.com/data-assumptions/): coordinates are perturbed by up to about 150 m, calendar unavailability does not identify bookings, and the data represent a snapshot. We do not use calendar-derived occupancy or revenue as an observed target.
- [Official dictionary](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1iWCNJcSutYqpULSQHlNyGInUvHg2BoUGoNRIGa6Szc4/edit): local CSV copy data/dictionary.csv. This public dictionary is version 4.3 (2022) and does not document all columns in the newer 90-column release. Its price definition is local-currency daily price; the dollar symbol in the CSV is an export artifact. The actual nested quote records were inspected to resolve the new schema.
- Raw quote audit: 59,761 valid nightly quote amounts, all matched by the displayed price within rounding; parsed quote currencies were GBP where supplied. Requested check-in/check-out dates vary. Amounts and raw quotes are excluded from predictors; date context is included. Price is an asking quote, not a completed-transaction price. A small number of price rows lack a parsed matching quote; those values rely on the dictionary's daily-price definition.
- [Gyódi and Nawaro (2021)](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.tourman.2021.104319): direct prior test of a centre-distance premium in ten European cities including London. Publisher abstract, hypothesis H2a and selected dataset excerpts were read; full text/tables were not. See literature/ for a provisional English summary and identity metadata. The idea is an established economic hypothesis and a recent replication, not an academic novelty claim.

No Kaggle competition entry is involved. This public alternative was chosen for direct access, a simple economic question and current data. No logins, messages, purchases, publication or competition submissions were performed.
