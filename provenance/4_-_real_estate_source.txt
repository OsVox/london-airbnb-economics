Implication of the research
The main goal of the research is to predict the price of the apartment
This study uses and compares different models in order to find the one that fits best to reach the goal above and
 
to find significant factors that have an influence on pricing on real estate market.
The research can be useful for two parties:
economists 
(
learning determinants of pricing on real estate market and instruments of estimation)
realtors (learning determinants of pricing, what mostly contributes to the price and what characteristics to make an attention of a buyer on; getting the prediction of fair prices on the market)
