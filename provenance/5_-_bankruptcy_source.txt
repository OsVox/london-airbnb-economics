WHY IS THIS IMPORTANT?
Purpose of our research
 was to find most optimal and effective model to predict the bankruptcy class.
Corporate bankruptcy risk assessment is important for creditors, investors and other stakeholders. It is important to prevent bankruptcy before the trial. 
The judicial procedure, especially initiated by creditors, carries risks of seizure of assets, loss of reputation, bringing controlling persons to subsidiary and criminal liability. 
Therefore, bankruptcy forecasting is an important area of research

STRATIFIED CROSS VALIDATION:
First setup (from presentation):
We have unbalanced target, therefore we implement StratifiedKfold with Shuffle split as it returns stratified randomized folds. The folds are made by preserving the percentage of samples for each class
Second setup (our version):
We add oversampling (SMOTE)  which oversample the examples in the minority class to balance the dataset.

BLENDING:
To achieve even better results, we tried to use blending.  We took random forest and logistic regression as the basic algorithms, since they are simple, but at the same time showed some of the best results. Light Gradient Boosting was chosen as a meta algorithm.
But unfortunately, due to the difficulty of selecting good hyperparameters in blending, we got an AUC score of only 0.86. This is also a good result, but it is not comparable with simpler models.

CONCLUSION:
In the course of our work, we used 2 approaches:
Improving an existing option by more detailed data processing, outliers, finding better parameters for models;
Feature selection using SelectKBest and data balancing using oversampling.
Both approaches were successful and we were able to beat last year's results. The second approach proved to be better due to greater suitability for dealing with imbalanced samples.
