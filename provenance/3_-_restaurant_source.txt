Set of task
 ● What can influence the preparation time of the food place?
   ○ Why: preparation time ~ quality of the place
   ○ Solution: linear model

 ● With what probability a customer will choose a restaurant over not-restaurant
   (sweets & bakes)?
    ○ Why: to make recommendations of food places more precise
    ○ Solution: logistic model

 ● How to recommend a certain place to eat?
   ○ Why: people always face this difficult choice
   ○ Solution: random forest
